Answer from the gut. First instinct, no deliberation, one answer.

Question: $ARGUMENTS

Format, and nothing else:

**Do this:** the concrete answer — file, shape, name, approach. Specific enough
to start typing from.

**Because:** one or two sentences of the real reason.

**If I'm wrong:** the cheapest signal that would tell me to turn around.

Under 150 words. No options, no trade-off table, no "it depends," no clarifying
questions — assume the most likely reading and say which assumption you made.

If the honest answer is that the question is the wrong one, say that and say what
the right question is.

One exception to answering from the gut: if any part of the answer is a
checkable fact — what a function returns, whether a flag exists — open the file
and check it. Instinct decides designs. It does not invent APIs.
