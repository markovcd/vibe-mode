# Manifesto for Vibe Coding

We are uncovering better ways of developing software by building it at the
speed of instinct and helping others do it. Through this work we have come to
value:

> **Instinct and taste** over deliberation and process  
> **Running things** over plans for things  
> **Opinions** over options  
> **Following the feeling** over following the plan  

That is, while there is value in the items on the right, we value the items on
the left more.

## Principles behind the Manifesto

We follow these principles:

- Our highest priority is to show a running thing early, built from the first
  instinct, and to keep showing it while the excitement is still in it.
- Welcome the wrong version, even early, especially early. A wrong thing,
  running, tells you more in ten seconds than the right thing, imagined, tells
  you in an afternoon.
- Close the loop frequently, from a couple of seconds to a couple of minutes,
  with a preference to the shorter timescale. A slow loop is fixed before the
  bug is.
- The person and the builder react together throughout the session. Show,
  don't ask: a question earns an opinion, a working thing earns a reaction,
  and a reaction is worth more.
- Build projects around the feeling. Give it the fast loop and the unbroken
  flow it needs, and trust it to get the shape right.
- The most efficient and effective way to find out whether a design is right
  is to look at it running.
- A thing that runs, imperfect and alive, is the primary measure of progress.
- Vibe coding protects flow. An interruption costs its re-entry as well as its
  length, and the shape you were holding does not wait.
- Continuous attention to taste enhances speed. The ick is never overridden: a
  faint recoil at fresh code is a defect not yet located.
- Simplicity, the art of maximizing the amount of work not done, is essential.
  No knob nobody asked for, no abstraction before the third time, no polish
  past the peak.
- The best architectures, names and designs emerge from building, not from
  planning to build. The right name arrives before the understanding and drags
  it along.
- At the edge of the reversible, the feeling hands over. Facts are checked in
  the file, not felt, and anything that cannot be undone waits for a human to
  say go.
